# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

NexTerm is for everyone who wants to use or improve it. We want participation to be a respectful, harassment-free experience regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully; critique the work, not the person.
- Keep discussion on topic and technical where possible.
- Respect different viewpoints and experience levels.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments, in public or private
- Personal or political attacks
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome advances
- Sustained disruption of discussions or deliberate spam
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer through the email on their GitHub profile, or open a private report from the repository's **Security** tab. Reports are handled privately and in good faith.
